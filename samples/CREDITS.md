# Sample clip credits

All three clips are trimmed, audio-stripped, re-encoded excerpts of U.S. Geological Survey
videos marked **Public Domain** on their USGS pages. The trims are ours. USGS does not
endorse this project.

| File | Length | Excerpt of | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `clip_01_drone_smoke_onset.mp4` | 30 s | 0:08–0:38 of "Prescribed Burn, Tall Timbers Research Station, FL (Drone)" | Top-down drone camera. Clear ground first, then smoke rolls in. Good for showing when the alert fires. |
| `clip_02_drone_flames_smoke.mp4` | 32 s | 0:44–1:16 of the same video | Top-down. Heavy smoke with small flame fronts visible through it. |
| `clip_03_ground_haze.mp4` | 22 s | 0:00–0:22 of "Prescribed Burn, Tall Timbers Research Station, FL (Footage of Drone)" | Ground-level view of the drone in dense haze. A hard case for smoke, and good for comparing CLAHE on and off. |

Sources (USGS Office of Communications, filmed 2017-04-19, Tall Timbers Research Station,
Tallahassee, Florida):

- <https://www.usgs.gov/media/videos/prescribed-burn-tall-timbers-research-station-fl-drone>
- <https://www.usgs.gov/media/videos/prescribed-burn-tall-timbers-research-station-fl-footage-drone>

Notes: the top-down clips come from a fisheye camera inside the drone's protective cage, so
curved cage bars are visible at the edges. Clips 01 and 02 are a prescribed (controlled)
burn, not a wildfire.
